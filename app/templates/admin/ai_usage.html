Why this ratio matters

The naive design calls the model on every tracked action, so this ratio would sit near 1. Here it is much higher because generation runs behind four gates (cooldown, momentum, behaviour-signature novelty, single-flight) and an unchanged behaviour signature serves the cached recommendation for free.
